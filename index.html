What changed for clay

  • Interior partitions go from 0.9 mm to 2.5 mm, the same single bead as the outside walls.
  • No overhangs. Anything that sits over a gap is removed: lintels over doors and windows go, so every opening runs up to the slab of the floor above, which closes it.
  • The stair and lift block prints as a hollow 2.5 mm shell, not a solid block.
  • Each floor prints its own slab first (5 layers, lines turned 90° each layer), then the walls straight on top.
  • Every wall is printed once. A stroke that ends against a wall already printed stops half a bead short, so the beads touch without piling up.
  • Widening the partitions makes each wall end about 0.8 mm longer, so doors close up by about 1.6 mm on the model.
  • Paths are drawn at final 1:100 size. Scale the G-code up by your measured shrinkage (about 112% for 11%).
